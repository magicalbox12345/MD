Privacy Policy
Last updated: 23.9.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how mAGICAL bOX handles data.

1. Data We Access
The App connects to TikTok via OAuth to obtain basic profile information (display name, avatar) solely to confirm which TikTok account is connected, and to publish video content on behalf of the account owner.

2. Data We Do Not Collect
The App does not collect, store, or share personal data from any TikTok users other than the operator's own connected account. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Storage
Access tokens are stored securely and used only to authorize publishing requests. Tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App from your TikTok account settings.

4. Third-Party Services
The App uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to publish content. No other third parties receive access to your TikTok data.
